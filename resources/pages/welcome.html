Choose a search engine

What's new in 2.0

Every part of the browser was redrawn. Here is where things are now.

Tab stripThe active tab is white with a red bar on top. The rest sit flat on the strip.
Address barOne field for search and addresses, with suggestions from your history.
More menuHistory, downloads, zoom and print live behind the three dots.
ExtensionsAdd Chrome extensions and see what each one may do before you add it.
ProfilesKeep work and personal browsing apart, each with its own bookmarks.
Site permissionsCamera, location and notification prompts say what the site wants.
Settings and themesEvery setting on one page, and themes that recolour the whole browser.
Sleeping tabsTabs you have not used for a while rest and free memory.
