# Pacitum Browser 2.0.0

Pacitum 2.0 is a complete redesign.

## ✨ New design
- **A new look** in light and dark: warm neutral colours, ink for buttons and switches, and Pacitum red for the active tab and other highlights.
- **New app icon** on Windows, macOS and Linux, and in the installer.
- **The PACITUM wordmark** on the new tab page and in About Pacitum, plus the **NEXT PROJECT** mark.
- **Rubik**, the new interface font, ships with Pacitum.
- **Taller tabs** with a red bar on the active tab, a roomier toolbar, a bigger address bar and bolder icons.
- **Sharper icons** at every screen scale (125 %, 150 % and so on): icons are drawn at the exact size of your screen.
- **Redrawn icons:** a proper puzzle-piece Extensions icon and new file-type icons in the Downloads list.

## ⚙️ Settings, rebuilt
- **A new Settings page** with a side list and a search box. It has 15 sections: You and Google, Passwords, Autofill, Privacy and security, Appearance, Themes, Search engine, Default browser, On startup, Downloads, Languages, Accessibility, System, Reset and About.
- **Themes:** Light, Dark, Midnight, Forest, Sakura, Sand, Graphite, High contrast, plus your own custom theme. A theme recolours the whole browser, and optionally the new tab page too.
- **Password manager:**
  - Saves, fills and suggests strong passwords.
  - Shows, copies and exports passwords only after Windows Hello or your Windows password.
  - Includes a password check for weak and reused passwords.
  - Imports and exports CSV.
- **Addresses and payment cards** are saved and filled into forms.
- **Site settings** let you set camera, microphone, location, notifications, pop-ups and more for each site, and reset a site.
- **Search shortcuts:** type `yt`, `w`, `gh` or `m` and a space to search YouTube, Wikipedia, GitHub or Maps.
- **HTTPS-only mode, third-party cookie blocking, languages and spellcheck, fonts, caret browsing (F7), run in the background, clear cookies on exit** and more.
- Pacitum can **restart** itself to apply a change.

## 🧩 Extensions page
- A new **Extensions** page (⋮ › Extensions › Manage extensions) lists Chrome extensions and user scripts with search, keyboard shortcuts and developer mode.
- Each extension has a details page with its permissions, size, version and source.

## 👤 Profile picker
- A new **profile picker** window lists each profile's email, open tabs and last use.
- It has a Manage mode, guest browsing and a "Show at startup" switch.

## 🏠 New tab page, menus and address bar
- **New tab page:**
  - The wordmark, date and time.
  - A bigger search box with an engine picker.
  - Top sites with visit counts.
  - "Where you left off" with Restore all.
  - Trackers blocked today and a Customise sheet.
- **⋮ menu:**
  - Your profile at the top.
  - A zoom row.
  - Sub-menus for History, Bookmarks, Extensions, More tools and Help, drawn as proper cards (they were solid black in early builds).
  - Print to PDF and Save page as.
- **Address bar suggestions** are larger and show what each row will do.
- **Tab strip:** dragging a tab keeps its red bar with it, and the sliding highlight only plays for a plain tab switch.

## 🪟 Web pop-ups and page dialogs
- **Sign-in windows** a site opens itself get their own small window. It always shows the real address, and **Open in a tab** moves the page into a tab.
- **alert, confirm and prompt**, "Leave this site?" and HTTP sign-in are drawn by Pacitum inside the tab. Only that tab is blocked, and you can stop a site from showing more dialogs.
- **Permission prompts** follow the new look.
- **Click a button twice to close its pop-up.** A second click on Extensions, Downloads, your profile, ⋮ or a pinned extension closes the pop-up instead of opening it again.

## 👋 Welcome
- **A new 7-step welcome:**
  1. Welcome.
  2. Pick light or dark, with a live preview.
  3. Import from another browser.
  4. Choose a search engine.
  5. What's new in 2.0.
  6. Turn on Google Drive sync.
  7. A summary of your choices.

## ⚡ Faster and steadier
- **No more blinking pop-ups:** menus and pop-ups fade in and close at once, with no left-over image when you click quickly.
- **Tab switching and Back/Forward no longer draw a cover image** over the page.
- **Pages no longer flash blank:** Chromium, the engine that draws web pages, used to pause and re-show the page whenever it thought another window was covering it. Pacitum now keeps the page drawing at all times.
- **Tab previews** for the tab overview are only kept up to date once you have used the overview, and never while a page is loading.
- **The tab's loading spinner repaints 30 times a second**, so loading pages put less load on the GPU.
- **Clicking a button on a page without a sign-in form no longer scans the page** for password fields.

## ℹ️ About Pacitum
- About Pacitum shows the new wordmark, your version and the **NEXT PROJECT** mark.
- **⋮ › Help and about Pacitum** opens it.

## Accent colour
Red is the new default accent. If you picked another accent colour in Settings, Pacitum keeps it.
