# Pacitum Browser 2.0.10

**The first full release of Pacitum 2.** Pacitum has a new design, a new Settings page, a password manager, Chrome extensions you control, its own language system, Linux packages, and a long list of fixes. If you're coming from 1.9.x, this page lists everything that changed. The newest parts come first.

---

## 🆕 New in 2.0.10

### ⌨️ Quick switcher (Ctrl+K)
- **Press Ctrl+K and type.** One box finds your open tabs, Pacitum's actions, your bookmarks and your history, and searches the web.
- **Open tabs come first.** Enter switches to a tab instead of opening the site again. Tabs that are asleep are marked.
- **Every action is listed with its shortcut:** new tab, private tab, split view, screenshot, reader mode, notes, zoom, full screen, themes, passwords, clear browsing data and more. English words work in every language.
- **Type an address** and the first row opens it. Plain words end with a web search.
- **Keyboard first:** ↑ ↓ to move, Enter to open, Esc (or Ctrl+K again) to close. It's also in the menu under More tools.
- **Private windows don't look through your history.** Results only use site icons Pacitum already has; listing them never downloads anything.

### 🌙 Dark websites
- **Settings › Appearance › Dark websites:** Off, With dark theme, or Always.
- **Sites that only come in light colours are shown dark**, using Chromium's own dark rendering. Sites with their own dark design keep it.
- **Applies at once** to every tab in every window. Pacitum's own pages keep their own design.

### 🧪 Tests that look at the screen
- **The start-up test checks that something is actually drawn.** A window that opens with an empty page area now fails.
- **New page test:** Pacitum's new tab page, a built-in test page and real sites (example.com and Wikipedia; YouTube, GitHub and Google as soft checks) must load, get a title and draw. It runs on Linux and Windows for every change.

### 🧹 Fixes
- **Fixed: on Fedora 42, and other systems with a newer brotli library, no web page ever drew.** The window opened, but Chromium's page process stopped at once: Pacitum's bundled copy of brotli clashed with the system's. The .deb, .rpm and AppImage now use the system's brotli. The new start-up test found this on its first run.
- **The update window no longer prints `QObject::disconnect` warnings on every start.** They buried real errors in the logs.

## Everything new since 1.9.x

### ✨ A new design (2.0)
- **A new look in light and dark:**
  - Warm neutral colours.
  - Ink-coloured buttons and switches.
  - Pacitum red for the active tab and other highlights.
- **A new app icon** on Windows and Linux and in the installer. **The PACITUM wordmark** appears on the new tab page and in About Pacitum, with the **NEXT PROJECT** mark.
- **Rubik**, the new interface font, ships with Pacitum.
- **Taller tabs** with a red bar on the active tab, a roomier toolbar, a bigger address bar and bolder icons.
- **Sharp at every scale:**
  - Icons are drawn at the exact size of your screen and placed on whole pixels, so they stay crisp at 125 % and 150 %.
  - On Windows, text in the toolbar, bookmarks bar, menus and pop-ups is snapped to whole pixels too.
  - Bookmark text is regular weight, no longer bold and blurry.
- **Redrawn icons**, including a proper puzzle-piece Extensions icon and file-type icons in Downloads.
- **Pop-ups and menus fade in and close at once**, with no blinking or left-over image. **Click a toolbar button again to close its pop-up.**

### 🎨 Themes and colours
- **15 themes**, 14 built in plus your own:
  - Light, Dark, Midnight, Forest, Sakura, Sand, Graphite and High contrast.
  - Ocean, Mint, Lavender, Sunset, Rose and Deep sea.
  - Your own custom theme.
- A theme recolours the whole browser, and optionally the new tab page.
- **Nine accent colours** (red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, indigo, purple and pink), plus each theme's own. Red is the default. An accent you picked before is kept.
- **Your custom theme stays** when you switch profiles or restart.
- **Dark websites** (new in 2.0.10, above).

### ⚙️ Settings, rebuilt
- **A new Settings page** with a grouped side list (You, Browsing, Look and feel, Pacitum), search, and a line under each section saying what it is for.
- **Sections:** You and Google, Passwords, Autofill, Privacy and security, Appearance, Themes, Search engine, Default browser, On startup, Downloads, Languages, Accessibility, System, Updates, Reset and About.
- **Site settings:** camera, microphone, location, notifications, pop-ups and more for each site, and a reset for each site.
- **Search shortcuts:** type `yt`, `w`, `gh` or `m` and a space to search YouTube, Wikipedia, GitHub or Maps.
- **More privacy and browsing options:** HTTPS-only mode, third-party cookie blocking, languages and spellcheck, fonts, text cursor browsing (F7), run in the background, and clear cookies on exit.
- **Tab size and tab overview style** in Appearance.
- Pacitum can **restart itself** to apply a change.

### 🔑 Passwords, addresses and cards
- **A built-in password manager:**
  - Saves and fills passwords, and suggests strong ones.
  - Finds weak and reused passwords.
  - Imports and exports CSV.
- **Showing, copying or exporting passwords asks for your computer password** (Windows Hello on Windows). Turning that check off needs the password too.
- **Addresses and payment cards** are saved and filled into forms.
- **Your passwords can't be wiped by a file that fails to open.** The file is kept aside instead of being overwritten. The same goes for addresses, cards and a damaged bookmarks file.

### 🌐 Your language
- **Pacitum speaks your language:** menus, dialogs, bubbles, the new tab page, Welcome and Settings.
- **Choose it in Settings › Languages › Display language**, or pick "Same as system". It applies at once, with no restart.
- **Thai (ไทย), Spanish (Español) and Japanese (日本語)** are included. Anything not translated yet stays in English.
- **Thai, Chinese, Japanese and Korean use a matching font** in menus and Pacitum pages.
- **No more garbled text like "à¸ à¸²à¸©à¸²".** Pacitum's pages are always read as UTF-8, and websites that don't name their encoding are read as UTF-8 too.

### 🗂️ Tabs, new tab page and browsing
- **New tab page:**
  - The wordmark, date and time.
  - A bigger search box with an engine picker.
  - Top sites with visit counts.
  - "Where you left off" with Restore all.
  - Trackers blocked today and a Customise sheet.
- **Tab overview (Ctrl+Shift+Space)** in a new dark design:
  - Carousel, Stack or Grid.
  - Card previews from the start of each page, including background tabs.
  - A count of open and sleeping tabs.
  - Keyboard hints.
- **Dragging tabs:**
  - The tab you drag stays selected and keeps its red bar.
  - Its title is no longer cut short after a drag.
  - The close button follows the selected tab straight away.
- **Bookmarks bar:**
  - Each bookmark shows its site's icon, fetched once for a site you haven't visited.
  - Folders with a drop-down list, "Open all", Move to folder and dragging.
  - Folders sync with Google sync.
- **⋮ menu:**
  - Your profile at the top.
  - A zoom row.
  - History, Bookmarks, Extensions, More tools and Help as proper sub-menus.
  - Print to PDF and Save page as.
- **History window (Ctrl+H):**
  - Search, open, remove entries or clear everything.
  - History saves the real page address and title, not the words you typed, and keeps the last year.
- **Address bar:**
  - Larger suggestions that say what each row will do.
  - `localhost:3000`, IP addresses, `mailto:` links and file paths open instead of being searched.
  - Search suggestions never receive addresses you type.
- **Find in page** has proper buttons and shows "2 of 5".
- **Shortcuts that work:** Ctrl+N (new window), Ctrl+R (reload) and Ctrl+U (page source). The menus showed them before, but they did nothing.
- **Sites without HTTPS can be opened:** with "Always use secure connections" on, a bar lets you continue to the insecure address.
- **Tracker blocking no longer breaks normal pages** (images named "banner", parcel tracking pages and similar).
- **Downloads:**
  - A program download asks once, not three times.
  - Two downloads of the same file no longer share one file.
  - Windows marks downloads as "from the internet" for SmartScreen.
- **The window opens no larger than your screen.**

### 🪟 Pop-ups, dialogs and the welcome
- **Sign-in windows a site opens get their own small window.** It always shows the real address, and **Open in a tab** moves the page into a tab.
- **alert, confirm, prompt, "Leave this site?" and HTTP sign-in** are drawn by Pacitum inside the tab. Only that tab waits, and you can stop a site from showing more dialogs.
- **Permission prompts** in the new design.
- **A new 7-step welcome:** pick light or dark with a live preview, import from another browser, choose a search engine, see what's new, and turn on Google Drive sync.
- **A new profile picker:**
  - Each profile's email, open tabs and last use.
  - Manage mode and guest browsing.
  - A "Show at startup" switch.

### 🕶️ Private tabs keep nothing
- Addresses you type in a private tab aren't saved to history.
- A closed private tab can't be reopened with Ctrl+Shift+T or from the new tab page.
- Downloads work in private tabs and aren't saved to the downloads list.
- Tracker blocking and "Do Not Track" work in private tabs.
- The "Private —" label stays on the tab, and the private badge shows only while a private tab is on screen.
- Private tabs never store site icons.

### 🧩 Chrome extensions and user scripts
- **Chrome extensions:**
  - Add them from the Chrome Web Store with **Add to Pacitum**.
  - Or add a `.crx`, a `.zip` or an unpacked folder.
  - Pin them to the toolbar.
- **Turning an extension on no longer crashes Pacitum.** Extensions keep their real Chrome ID.
- **An extension page** (⋮ › Extensions › Manage extensions):
  - Search, keyboard shortcuts and developer mode.
  - A details page with each extension's permissions, size, version and source.
- **Site access you control:**
  - **When I click it:** the extension waits until you click its button on a site.
  - **On sites I choose:** it runs only on the sites you list.
  - **On all sites:** as installed.
  - An extension never gets a site it didn't ask for.
- **User scripts can be allowed in private tabs** (off by default). Chrome extensions don't run in private tabs yet.
- **Extension pop-ups:**
  - Open reliably, including Enhancer for YouTube.
  - Links in a pop-up open in a tab.
  - A button without a pop-up opens the extension's options.
- **Tampermonkey works.** Pacitum crashed reading icons that come in several sizes.
- **A crashing extension can't lock you out of a profile.**
  - If a profile keeps closing right after it starts, Pacitum moves the newest extension aside and tells you which one.
  - An extension that has been working fine is never turned off by mistake.
- **An extension from the Chrome Web Store must have the ID you asked for**, or it isn't added.

### 🎬 Video
- **H.264 video and AAC audio play:** YouTube live streams, Twitch, news sites, video embeds and most MP4 files. Live streams used to say "Can't play this video".

### 🔄 Updates
- **Update channels** in Settings › Updates:
  - **Stable** (recommended) or **Beta**.
  - Beta offers GitHub pre-releases a few weeks early, after you confirm.
  - Switching back to Stable never downgrades.
- **The updater only installs real Pacitum releases:**
  - Downloaded over HTTPS from GitHub.
  - Checked against their SHA-256 checksum.
  - Other files on the release page are never mistaken for an update.
- **Clicking "Update" no longer closes Pacitum.** If the update helper can't start, Setup opens with its own window.
- **The Linux update keeps the old version** if the new one can't be put in place.
- **A package install takes the right file:** a Pacitum installed from a .deb or .rpm downloads the .deb or .rpm, and the AppImage updates itself.

### 🐧 Linux
- **.deb and .rpm packages** for Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Fedora and others:
  - A menu entry and a `pacitum-browser` command.
  - Can be set as the default browser.
  - Install with `sudo apt install ./…deb` or `sudo dnf install ./…rpm`.
  - The AppImage is still available.
- **The packages open on more systems:**
  - They bring an AppArmor profile, which Ubuntu 24.04, Mint 22, Pop!_OS and Zorin need for Chromium's sandbox.
  - They install every library Pacitum uses: OpenSSL, system certificates, sound, brotli and more.
  - They refuse to install on a system that's too old, instead of installing a browser that can't open.
- **KDE Plasma and other Wayland desktops:** the window and pages draw properly. The part of Qt that gives Wayland windows OpenGL was missing.
- **Fedora 42:** pages draw (new in 2.0.10).
- **If the browser still can't start, it retries in a safe mode** (software rendering, or no sandbox only where the system doesn't allow it). It tells you which library is missing, and keeps a log at `~/.local/share/Pacitum/Pacitum Browser/launch.log`. `PACITUM_SAFE_MODE=1` starts straight in safe mode.
- **The menu icon is installed in every size**, so it's sharp in the menu and task bar.
- **Showing saved passwords asks for your computer password.**

### 🛟 Safer data and steadier running
- **Sync:**
  - Can't empty your bookmarks when Google Drive comes back empty or you sign in with another account.
  - No longer gets stuck "syncing".
  - Its sign-in is stored encrypted.
- **Clearing data:**
  - "Clear when Pacitum closes" waits for the last window.
  - "Clear cookies" also clears what sites store.
- **Two windows no longer overwrite each other's open tabs or notes.**
- **Bookmarks are saved safely**, so a crash while saving can't damage the file.
- **Fixed crashes:**
  - "QR code for this page" crashed on very long addresses.
  - Clicking "Update" crashed Pacitum.
- **Smoother:**
  - Animations run at your screen's refresh rate, up to 240 Hz.
  - With 16 GB of memory or more, busy tabs slow the others down less.
  - Pages no longer flash blank, and switching tabs no longer draws a cover image over the page.
- **Crash reports:** after an unexpected close, Pacitum asks once whether to send a report. Addresses are cut to the site name, and user names and e-mail addresses are removed. You can change this in Settings › System. **Help › Report a problem** sends your description with the session log.
- **Logs work for every Windows user name**, including Thai and other non-Latin names.

### 🏷️ Platforms and publisher
- **Windows and Linux only.**
  - macOS is no longer built (since 2.0.4). Existing macOS installs keep working but get no updates.
  - The legacy 1.8.x edition is no longer built.
- **Next PRJ** is named as the publisher in the installer, the app and the Linux packages.
