# Pacitum Browser 2.0.10

One box to find anything, dark colours for every website, and tests that check Pacitum really draws pages, not only that its window opens.

## ⌨️ Quick switcher (Ctrl+K)
- **Press Ctrl+K and type.** One box finds your open tabs, Pacitum's actions, your bookmarks and your history, and searches the web.
- **Open tabs come first.** Enter switches to a tab instead of opening the site again. Tabs that are asleep are marked.
- **Every action, with its shortcut:** new tab, private tab, split view, screenshot, reader mode, notes, zoom, full screen, themes, passwords, clear browsing data and more. Typing in English works in every language.
- **Type an address** and the first row opens it. Plain words end with a web search.
- **Keyboard first:** ↑ ↓ to move, Enter to open, Esc (or Ctrl+K again) to close. Also in the menu under More tools.
- **Private windows don't look through your history.** Listing results never downloads site icons; only icons Pacitum already has are shown.

## 🌙 Dark websites
- **Settings › Appearance › Dark websites:** Off, With dark theme, or Always.
- **Sites that only come in light colours are shown in dark colours**, using Chromium's own dark rendering. Sites with their own dark design keep it.
- **Applies at once** to every tab in every window. Pacitum's own pages keep their own design.

## 🧪 Tests that look at the screen
- **The start-up test now checks that something is drawn.** A window that opens with an empty page area fails. The KDE Plasma / Wayland problem fixed in 2.0.9-A passed the old test.
- **New page test:** Pacitum's new tab page, a built-in test page and real sites (example.com, Wikipedia; YouTube, GitHub and Google as soft checks) must load, get a title and draw, on Linux and Windows.

## 🧹 Fixes
- The update window no longer prints `QObject::disconnect: wildcard call…` warnings on every start, which buried real errors in the logs.
