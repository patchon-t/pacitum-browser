# Pacitum Browser 2.0.7

YouTube live streams, Twitch and MP4 video now play.

## 🎬 Video that plays
- **Fixed "Can't play this video" on YouTube live streams** ([#52](https://github.com/patchon-t/pacitum-browser/issues/52)). Live streams send their sound only as AAC, which Pacitum's video engine couldn't play.
- **Pacitum now plays H.264 video and AAC audio.** These are the most common formats on the web, so Twitch, news sites, video embeds and most MP4 files work too.
- Works on Windows and Linux (installer, .deb, .rpm and AppImage).

## 🔧 Under the hood
- The updater only offers Pacitum versions. Other files published on the project's release page are never mistaken for an update.

Thanks to everyone who reported the problem. Keep reporting bugs with `/bug` on our Discord.
