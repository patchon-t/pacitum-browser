# Pacitum Browser 2.0.1

A stability and quality update. Pacitum now speaks your language, and icons and text are sharper.

## 🌐 Language system
- **Pacitum follows your language.** Menus, dialogs, bubbles, the new tab page, Welcome and Settings all use your Windows / system language by default.
- **Choose it yourself** in Settings › Languages › Display language. The change applies at once, with no restart; open Pacitum pages reload in the new language.
- Anything that has no translation yet stays in English, so nothing goes missing.
- Pacitum's own pages and the browser menus use the same language, including sentences with numbers such as "3 tabs are asleep".
- Thai is included in this version.

## 🔎 Sharper icons and text
- Toolbar, window-button and tab-strip icons are now placed on whole screen pixels, so they stay crisp at 125 % and 150 % scaling instead of looking soft.
- Better font fallbacks for Thai, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, so those scripts are no longer drawn in a mismatched face.

## 🛠 Stability
- Fixes to the black "More tools" submenu, tab-strip drag glitches and pages that flashed blank.
