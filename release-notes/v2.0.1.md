# Pacitum Browser 2.0.1

A stability and quality update. Pacitum now speaks your language, and icons and text are sharper.

## 🌐 Language system
- **Pacitum speaks your language.** Menus, dialogs, bubbles, the new tab page, Welcome and Settings can all be shown in another language. English is the default.
- **Choose it** in Settings › Languages › Display language (or pick "Same as system"). The change applies at once, with no restart; open Pacitum pages reload in the new language.
- Anything that has no translation yet stays in English, so nothing goes missing.
- Pacitum's own pages and the browser menus use the same language, including sentences with numbers such as "3 tabs are asleep".
- This version includes **Thai (ไทย), Spanish (Español) and Japanese (日本語)**; more languages will follow.

## 🔎 Sharper icons and text
- Toolbar, window-button and tab-strip icons are now placed on whole screen pixels, so they stay crisp at 125 % and 150 % scaling instead of looking soft.
- Thai, Chinese, Japanese and Korean now use a matching Windows font (Leelawadee UI, Microsoft YaHei UI, Yu Gothic UI, Malgun Gothic) in menus, dialogs and Pacitum pages instead of a mismatched default face or missing letters.

## 🛠 Stability
- Fixes to the black "More tools" submenu, tab-strip drag glitches and pages that flashed blank.
