# Pacitum Browser 2.0.6

Thai and other languages display correctly everywhere, Settings get a new look with a Beta update channel, the tab overview is redesigned, text is sharper, and there are new colour themes.

## 🔤 Thai and other languages display correctly
- **Fixed garbled text such as "à¸ à¸²à¸©à¸²" in Settings, the new tab page and tab titles.** On some computers Pacitum's own pages were read with the wrong encoding, so translated text turned into strange letters. Pacitum now always says its pages are UTF-8.
- **Websites that don't name their encoding are read as UTF-8**, so Thai, Japanese and other non-Latin text shows properly instead of as symbols.

## ⚙️ Redesigned Settings
- The sidebar is grouped into **You**, **Browsing**, **Look and feel** and **Pacitum**, with the version at the bottom.
- Each section opens with an icon and a line saying what it is for.
- **New Updates section** with the update status, the update channel and update options.

## 🧪 Beta update channel
- **Settings › Updates › Update channel**: choose **Stable** (recommended) or **Beta**.
- Beta also offers GitHub pre-releases, a few weeks before Stable. Pacitum asks you to confirm first, because beta builds are tested less.
- On Beta, the newest pre-releases and their notes are listed.
- Switching back to Stable never downgrades: Pacitum waits for the next stable version newer than your beta, and your data stays.

## 🃏 New tab overview (Ctrl + Shift + Space)
- A dark, focused view that shows how many tabs are open and how many are asleep.
- Three styles: **Carousel**, **Stack** and **Grid**. Switch at the top, press 1 / 2 / 3, or choose in **Settings › Appearance › Tab overview style**.
- Each card shows the tab's title bar and a preview. The current tab is marked.
- Arrows, position dots and keyboard hints at the bottom.

## ✨ Sharper text
- **On Windows, text in the toolbar, bookmarks bar, menus and pop-ups is snapped to whole pixels.** It no longer looks soft or blurry, especially at 125% and 150% display scaling.

## 🎨 More colours
- **Six new colour themes:** Ocean, Mint, Lavender, Sunset, Rose and Deep sea, alongside Midnight, Forest, Sakura, Sand, Graphite and High contrast.
- **Ten accent colours:** red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, indigo, purple and pink, plus each theme's own.
